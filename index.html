A Letter From Your Nageena 💌

Happiest Birthday, my Hemuuuu! ❤️🫂🎂

Hemuuu, where should I even start? When I think about our friendship, I always feel so happy remembering how everything started. It's actually so special to think about how a person whom I barely knew during our first year became such an important part of my life. 🥹💗

I still remember seeing you for the first time in our Inter first-year online classes on Zoom. You used to ask doubts in the classes, and I remember thinking, "OMG, she's asking so many questions!" I had already noticed you as one of the toppers in our online classes. Then, when I came to college, I remember seeing your name on the board after our JEE Main test, where you had secured one of the top positions. I hadn't even seen you during the first week, but I had already seen your name on that board. My roommate, who was your classmate, also used to tell me about you and how you were a topper. I think I actually saw you around the third week.

But honestly, during my first year of Inter, I didn't pay much attention to you. You used to come and talk to my parents whenever they visited, while I barely used to talk to you. My parents actually knew you better than I did at that time. Little did I know that one day you would become someone so close to my heart. ❤️🧿

And then came our second year. That's when we actually started becoming close. Hemuuu, I can never forget that one incident when I fell sick. You came to my room, and I told you that I had a fever. I even asked you to leave my room. You went away, but when I woke up, I saw you sleeping beside me. 🥺❤️

Actually, that was a time when I felt like not many people cared about me, but you cared so much. You always made me feel that someone was there for me. I will never forget the way you cared for me during those days. 🫂💗

I don't know whether you remember that moment the same way I do, but for me, it was something very special. At that moment, I felt that you were a true friend. Someone who genuinely cared about me. I felt so loved and cared for without you even having to say anything. That was the moment I decided that I never wanted to lose you at any cost. I don't think you will ever understand how much that little moment meant to me. 🥹❤️

And after that, we became so close. Our EAMCET preparation days at home, those endless conversations, chatting for hours and hours, sharing every little thing and never getting tired of talking to each other. I miss those days so much, Hemuuu. ❤️✨

I seriously don't know how time passed so quickly. We were once those two girls who could talk for hours without even realising how much time had passed. And suddenly, we reached B.Tech. A completely new life, new responsibilities, new friends and so many things to take care of. Now we don't get enough time to talk like we used to during our EAMCET preparation. Sometimes we don't even get time to have a proper conversation. But Hemuuu, please remember that even though our conversations have become less, my love for you has never become less. 🥺💕

Every day, in some way or the other, I remember you. Whenever I feel low, your words come to my mind. The things you said to me, the way you encouraged me and the way you cared for me always stay with me. Sometimes, even when you are not around, your words make me feel better. That's how special you are to me. ❤️🫂

You are my one and only true best friend, Hemuuu. Whether you are beside me or far away, whether we talk every day or don't talk for days, you will always be my bestest friend. Your place in my life is something I can never give to anyone else. No matter how many people I meet or how many friends I make, nobody can take your place. It's always going to be yours. ❤️🧿

And Hemuuu, I really want to say sorry for all the times I have hurt you during B.Tech. I know how many times I told you that I would call you back or text you, but I never did. I know it's my mistake. Every time, I tell you that I won't repeat it again, but somehow I end up doing the same thing and hurting you. I am really, really sorry for that. You never deserved to feel that I was ignoring you or that I didn't have time for you. 🥺

But please, one thing I want to tell you is that even if I don't call or text you sometimes, please don't leave me. Please don't think that I don't care about you just because I don't call or reply. It's never like that, Hemuuu. Calls and texts are different, but you are always there in my thoughts. I might not express it every day, but I always think about you. I don't want to lose you over these little things. I want you to understand me, just like I always want to understand you. I promise that no matter how busy life gets, you will always have a special place in my heart. 🥹❤️🫂

And you know what? Whenever my B.Tech friends talk about their best friends or introduce their best friends, your name is always the first one that comes to my mind. If someone asks me who my best friend is, I will always say your name first. Even if you are not physically with me, you are still the first person I think of when someone asks about my best friend. That's how much you mean to me. 💗

And now, coming to our birthday story! ❤️🎂

I still remember how I completely forgot your birthday during my first year of B.Tech and accidentally wished you on November 11. I really can't believe I did that! Then, during my second year, I tried so hard to remember your birthday. I told all my friends to remind me, even asked my parents to set a reminder, and finally managed to wish you at exactly 12:00 AM. And now, here I am in my fourth year, remembering your birthday all by myself! Nobody helped me, nobody reminded me, and I'm wishing you at exactly 12:00 AM. I think finally I have become a little responsible when it comes to your birthday! ❤️🥹

But honestly, I really wanted to remember your birthday on my own this time and wish you exactly at 12. And I'm so happy that I did. You deserve to be remembered, celebrated and loved, not just on your birthday but every single day. 🌷💗

Hemuuu, I don't know what life has planned for both of us. We may get busy with our careers, move to different places, meet new people and enter different phases of life. But one thing I really wish is that we never become distant in our hearts. I want us to stay like this forever. I want to hear about your happiness, your problems, your achievements, your silly stories and every little thing happening in your life. Even after years, I want us to sit together and talk about our Inter days and remember how everything started. 🥹❤️

Thank you for coming into my life. Thank you for caring for me when I needed someone the most. Thank you for all those hours of conversations, for every little thing you did for me and for making so many beautiful memories with me. You might think some of those things are very small, but I carry them in my heart. 💗🫂

You are not just a friend I made during Inter, Hemuuu. You are someone I want to keep in my life forever. ❤️♾️

I wish you all the happiness in the world. May all your dreams come true, may you achieve everything you have ever wished for, and may you always have a reason to smile. You deserve so much love, happiness and success. 🌸🧿💕

And lastly, please never forget that no matter how many days we go without talking, no matter how busy we become and no matter where life takes us, you will always be my favourite person and my one and only best friend. 🥹❤️

I love you so much, Hemuuuu! More than I can ever explain in words. 🥹❤️🫂

Happiest Birthday once again, my forever best friend! 🎂💗✨

Lots and lots of love,
Your Nageena! ❤️🫂
